MARKING_PREP
Creates closed marking boundaries from horizontal lightweight polylines with positive constant width. Choose continuous or dashed; dashed output needs positive dash and gap lengths. Unsupported paths are kept for review.

MARKING_SOLIDS
Extrudes marking boundaries into 3D solids using a selected Civil 3D surface. Requires valid closed boundaries and a surface; nested boundaries become cutouts.

MARKING_BLOCK
Combines all Model Space 3D solids on the selected solid's layer into one block, based at the origin.

MARKING_BLOCK_LAYER
Moves selected block references to an existing or new layer. Optionally changes their contents' layers; dynamic blocks and external references are skipped.
