MARKING_PREP
Creates closed marking boundaries from horizontal lightweight polylines with positive constant width. Choose continuous or dashed; dashed output needs positive dash and gap lengths. Unsupported paths are kept for review.

MARKING_PREP_BATCH
Prepares a selection or current-space groups summarized by layer, linetype, and width. Ctrl/Shift-select group rows and apply continuous or dashed output plus positive dash and gap lengths to multiple groups. Toggling Include on one selected row applies that state to all selected rows. Continuous output clears and disables that row's dash/gap cells. Invalid geometry and varying widths are summarized; overlaps are checked among included groups. Exceptions are retained for review, with a confirmation before successful source polylines are erased.

MARKING_CURVES
Converts selected standalone horizontal circles and full ellipses to closed lightweight polylines. Enter a shared positive marking width in drawing units. Circles use three exact arc segments; ellipses use a requested chord-deviation tolerance. Choose whether to keep or delete the source curves. Unsupported curves are retained.

MARKING_SOLIDS
Extrudes marking boundaries into 3D solids using a selected Civil 3D surface. Requires valid closed boundaries and a surface. When nested boundaries are detected, choose the existing annular solid, an annular solid plus an inner solid with separate thickness, or skip the nested group. Open or non-horizontal polylines are skipped while other valid boundaries continue. An open polyline and a closed boundary with containing extents at the same elevation are skipped together as a possibly incomplete annular pair.

MARKING_BLOCK
Combines all Model Space 3D solids on the selected solid's layer into one block, based at the origin.

MARKING_BLOCK_LAYER
Moves selected block references to an existing or new layer. Optionally changes their contents' layers; dynamic blocks and external references are skipped.
